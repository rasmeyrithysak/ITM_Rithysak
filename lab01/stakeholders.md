# Stakeholders: Realtime Quiz

The two stakeholders with high influence and high interest are the sponsor (S01, Head of the IT department) and the project manager (S02, Tal Tongsreng). The sponsor controls the budget and decides at each gate whether the project continues, so the PM keeps them informed first, with a clear demo in week 7 and a pilot report in week 14. The PM works with the team daily and approves most decisions, so the two of them must agree on scope and changes early.

The veto stakeholder is the data protection officer (S09): the pilot cannot run without approval to store student names and results, so a one-page data description must be sent well before week 13.
